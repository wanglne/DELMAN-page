### Why Do Deployed Models Need Safety Updates?

Models may undergo foundational and downstream safety alignment before reaching users, yet new jailbreak attempts can still emerge during deployment. DELMAN addresses these threats through localized weight updates, allowing further safety edits as new attacks are identified.

<h2 id="methodology-title">Methodology</h2>

### How Does DELMAN Edit a Model?

<PaperFigure name="method" alt="Five-step DELMAN editing process: identify harmful tokens, generate varied contexts, average harmful key representations, optimize safe values with utility regularization, and update MLP down-projection weights." />

<details className="evidence-details">
  <summary><span>Optimization and Weight Update</span><span>Eqs. (5) and (8)</span></summary>
  <div className="details-body">
    <p>The target value objective combines safe refusal supervision with a KL regularizer for benign prompts:</p>

$$
v^* = \arg\min_v\left[\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{safe}} + \lambda\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{utility}}\right].
$$

    <p>The MLP update encodes the new key–value mappings while using cached covariance statistics to preserve existing associations:</p>

$$
\widehat{W}_{\mathrm{down}} = W_{\mathrm{down}} + R_D K_D^\top\left(C + K_D K_D^\top\right)^{-1}.
$$

    <p className="table-note">In practice, updates are distributed across selected layers.</p>
  </div>
</details>

### Can Safety Edits Accumulate over Time?

DELMAN applies four safety edits sequentially, each targeting a different harmful behavior category. In both cases, overall HarmBench ASR decreases, while previously edited categories maintain low ASR after subsequent updates.
